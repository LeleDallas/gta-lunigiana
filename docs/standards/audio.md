# Audio standards

Checks for a change in `src/audio`, on top of the steps in
[CODING_STANDARDS.md](../../CODING_STANDARDS.md):

- **Levels**: tune `src/audio/director/levels.ts` by measurement (`measure.ts`, the crash tests).
- **New sound kind**: "Render all" on the audition page (`lab.html`, or `window.lab.renderAll()`)
  flags no row.
- **Tests table**: keep the tests table in [audio.md](../audio.md#tests) in step with the tests.
